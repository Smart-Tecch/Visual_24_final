-----------------------------------------------------
PART 0 - GENERAL RULES FOR EVERY PICTURE
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Orientation: LANDSCAPE (wider than tall) for hero, cards and banners.
2. Size: at least 1600 px wide for normal pictures, at least 2400 px wide for the hero.
3. Quality: sharp, bright, no blur, no watermarks, no text written on the picture.
4. Faces: friendly, smiling, real people. Students and teachers look best.
5. Look for Black/African students and young people so visitors see themselves.
6. Keep one colour mood (pick blue, or dark with orange accents) so the site feels consistent.
7. File type: convert to .webp before use (use squoosh.app, it is free). Aim for under 300 KB each.
8. Leave empty space in the hero picture (left or right side) so the text is easy to read.
9. Do not use one picture twice on the same page.
10. Best of all: later replace these with real photos of YOUR students and classroom.
    Real photos build more trust with parents than any stock picture.

-----------------------------------------------------
PART 8 - HOW TO DOWNLOAD FROM PINTEREST
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Open the pin you like.
2. Tap the three dots (...) then "Download image".
3. For a sharper copy: click the pin's source link and download from the original website.
4. Pinterest often gives small pictures (about 736 px wide). Small pictures look
   blurry when stretched across a hero. If it looks soft, find a bigger one or
   use the free sites in Part 9.
5. Rename the file, convert it to .webp at squoosh.app, check it is under 300 KB.


-----------------------------------------------------
PART 9 - LICENSE WARNING AND SAFER SOURCES
-----------------------------------------------------
Most Pinterest pictures belong to someone else. Using them on a school website
that earns money can cause copyright complaints. Safer plan:
  - Use Pinterest to FIND the style you like (save it to a board as inspiration).
  - Then download the same kind of picture from FREE-TO-USE sites:
      * unsplash.com
      * pexels.com
      * pixabay.com
    Use the same search phrases from this list on those sites.
  - Check the license before you download. These three allow free use on websites.
